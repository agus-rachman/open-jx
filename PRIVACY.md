# Privacy

open-jx is a set of instructions and reference files that run inside your
Microsoft Copilot Cowork tenant. It has no servers, collects no data, makes
no network calls of its own and includes no connectors. Your documents and
conversations are handled by Microsoft 365 under your organisation's agreement
with Microsoft.

The eval suite's fixtures are synthetic. Do not commit real client documents or
transcripts to this repository.
